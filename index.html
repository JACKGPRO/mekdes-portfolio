A bit about me ★

ABOUT ME

I’m Mekdes, a barista and front-of-house professional based in London. Over the past three years I have worked across cafés, restaurants, a fitness studio and a property staging business in London and Canada. I’m currently at Whiskers & Cream, where I rotate between the bar, the floor and the kitchen depending on what the day needs.

Coffee is the part of the job I take the most pride in. I dial in the espresso machine each morning and keep adjusting it through the day so every shot stays consistent. Before Whiskers & Cream I spent a year and a half with Blank Street Coffee, working across several London stores and representing the brand at events including London Fashion Week.

I’m just as comfortable on a front desk. At SoulCycle Soho I ran check-in across high-demand classes, handled member enquiries by phone and email and looked after the day-to-day admin. Wherever I have worked I have been the first person customers deal with, and I have learned to stay organised and calm when it gets busy, to pick up new systems quickly and to build the kind of rapport that brings people back.
