The Journey

MY STORY ★

★ 2022

Small-Town Canada

Cashier — The Ice Cream Shop, Bouctouche, NB

Growing up in a small town in New Brunswick, I learned the fundamentals of customer service the old-fashioned way — face to face, one person at a time. At The Ice Cream Shop, I built genuine rapport with regulars, drove repeat business through personalised service, and discovered that every single customer interaction tells you something about what people want and how they want to feel. That foundation — reading people, making them feel seen — has shaped everything I've done since.

★ 2023

Crossed the Atlantic

Waitress — The Cliff Poole, Bournemouth

At 18, I made the decision to leave Canada and move to the UK entirely on my own — self-funded, no safety net. I landed in Bournemouth and threw myself into waitressing at The Cliff Poole, a high-volume restaurant where the pace never let up. I learned how to coordinate under pressure, communicate clearly with a kitchen team, and deliver consistent service when the room was full and the clock was against you. Moving across the world alone taught me grit. Working The Cliff taught me how to channel it.

★ 2024

Found Blank Street

Barista — Blank Street Coffee, London Soho

London is where everything clicked. I joined Blank Street Coffee in Soho and immediately connected with the brand — the energy, the design, the way it speaks to its audience. Over nearly two years I've worked across multiple high-traffic locations, preparing drinks at speed while maintaining the quality and warmth that keeps people coming back. But more than that, I started paying attention to the marketing — the packaging, the campaigns, the way customers respond to seasonal launches. I see the brand from the inside out, every single day.

★ 2025–26

The Next Chapter

Front Desk — SoulCycle + Blank Street Coffee

While continuing at Blank Street, I also picked up a role at SoulCycle — another brand-led, community-driven company where the customer experience is the marketing. Between the two, I've built a deep understanding of how lifestyle brands create loyalty, how physical spaces translate brand identity, and how the people on the front line are the ones who truly shape customer perception. Now I'm ready to take everything I've learned behind the counter and bring it to the campaign table.

WHY ME

I've spent nearly two years watching how Blank Street's customers behave, what draws them in, what makes them come back, and what falls flat.

Marketing isn't just about campaigns — it's about understanding the gap between what a brand promises and what a customer actually experiences. I've stood in that gap thousands of times. I know your demographic because I serve them, I talk to them, I see what resonates.

I'm ready to take that perspective off the shop floor and into the marketing team — where I can help shape the story I've been part of for two years.
